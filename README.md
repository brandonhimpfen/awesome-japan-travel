Japan is a fascinating East Asian destination seamlessly blending ultra-modern urban innovation with ancient Shinto shrines, serene Zen gardens, and mountainous volcanic national parks. This list is intended for cultural travelers, rail-transit explorers, culinary tourists, and photography enthusiasts.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, e-Visa systems, and pre-arrival clearance platforms.

- [Official Japan Travel Guide (JNTO)](https://www.japan.travel/en/) - Comprehensive national tourism portal featuring destination guides, itineraries, seasonal highlights, and practical visitor info.
- [Visit Japan Web (Digital Agency)](https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/) - Official pre-arrival web service for streamlining immigration, customs declarations, and tax-free shopping QR codes.
- [Japan eVISA System (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/visaonline.html) - Official platform for online short-term tourist visa applications for eligible nationalities.
- [Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA)](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/indexe.html) - Official national meteorological agency providing weather forecasts, typhoon tracking, and seismic activity alerts.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating Japan Railways (JR), Shinkansen bullet trains, domestic air routes, and IC transit cards.

- [Japan Rail Pass Official Site](https://japanrailpass.net/) - Official booking and pricing portal for nationwide and regional JR rail passes.
- [JR-EAST Train Reservation](https://www.jreast.co.jp/) - Online booking platform for seat reservations on Shinkansen and express trains across Eastern Japan.
- [HyperDia / Jorudan Route Finder](https://world.jorudan.co.jp/) - Essential multi-modal transit planners for mapping train schedules, platforms, and fares across national networks.
- [Japan Airlines (JAL)](https://www.jal.co.jp/jp/en/) - National airline carrier offering domestic explorer air passes and international gateway flights.
- [All Nippon Airways (ANA)](https://www.ana.co.jp/en/jp/) - Major domestic and international carrier connecting regional airports across all major Japanese islands.

## Destinations & Regions

### Tokyo & Kanto

- [Go Tokyo (Official Tokyo Travel Guide)](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/) - Official municipal tourism portal for exploring Tokyo's neighborhoods, museums, dining, and transit networks.
- [Tokyo Metro Official Site](https://www.tokyometro.jp/en/) - Official subway map, route planner, and ticketing guide for navigating central Tokyo.
- [JNTO Hakone & Mount Fuji Guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kanagawa/hakone/) - Visitor portal for hot spring ryokans, lake cruises, and iconic views of Mount Fuji.

### Kyoto, Osaka & Kansai

- [Kyoto Travel Official Guide](https://kyoto.travel/en/) - Official municipal portal for exploring historic temples, shrines, bamboo groves, and traditional districts.
- [Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau](https://osaka-info.jp/en/) - Visitor guide for Dotonbori gastronomy, Osaka Castle, and regional Kansai attractions.
- [Nara Prefecture Tourism](https://www.visitnara.jp/) - Official travel hub for Nara Park, ancient temples, and free-roaming sika deer.

### Hokkaido & Northern Nature

- [Official Hokkaido Tourism Portal](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/en/) - Comprehensive regional guide for Daisetsuzan national park, Sapporo snow festivals, and coastal nature.

### Kyushu & Western Japan

- [Visit Kyushu Official Tourism Site](https://www.visit-kyushu.com/) - Regional travel guide for Fukuoka's food culture, Beppu hot springs, and active volcanoes in Aso.
- [Hiroshima Official Tourism Guide](https://dive-hiroshima.com/en/) - Visitor portal for the Peace Memorial Park, Miyajima Island, and Seto Inland Sea heritage.

## Connectivity & Navigation

Mapping tools, public Wi-Fi access, and digital transit integration.

- [Japan Travel by NAVITIME](https://www.navitime.co.jp/pcstorage/html/english_navitime/) - Comprehensive offline-capable navigation app featuring transit routing, Wi-Fi spots, and destination guides.
- [Google Maps Japan](https://map.google.com/) - Essential mapping platform optimized for real-time train platform numbers, bus schedules, and walking directions.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on disaster prevention apps, emergency hotlines, and medical assistance.

- [Safety Tips App (JNTO)](https://www.jnto.go.jp/safety-tips/eng/) - Official push-notification app providing early earthquake warnings, tsunami alerts, and weather advisories in English.
- [Japan National Tourism Organization Tourist Hotline](https://www.jnto.go.jp/) - 24-hour multi-language emergency support helpline for travelers (**050-3816-2787**).
- [Emergency Numbers (Police 110 / Ambulance & Fire 119)](https://www.japan.gov.jp/) - Direct emergency dispatch lines operational across all prefectures.
